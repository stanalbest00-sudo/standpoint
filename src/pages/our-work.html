Standpoint Seychelles brings people together to discuss the decisions shaping our islands — through live podcasts, street interviews and youth forums.

Who we are

Conversations for every Seychellois

We ask what public issues mean for everyday life in Seychelles, and we invite people to hear different perspectives and bring their own questions.

Our audience is people across Seychelles, with particular attention to young Seychellois and future voters.

Our story

Milestones

  1. 8 December 2025
    Founding

    First recorded planning meeting

    At Hebrews Café, the founding team discussed the purpose of Standpoint Seychelles, elected its initial officers and outlined a weekly live podcast. A central concern was how to introduce civic issues to young people before they vote for the first time.

  2. 27 May 2026
    Public launch

    Standpoint Seychelles launches

    Standpoint Seychelles officially launched at Hebrews Café, Link Building, Ile du Port. Founded by Apostle Jude Fred, the initiative brought together a youth committee, chaired at launch by Carlos Marianne.

    {{i:news}}Seychelles Nation, 30 May 2026“New civic movement empowers Seychellois youth” {{i:youtube}}Watch the launchOfficial launch video on our YouTube channel
  3. 26 June 2026
    Youth forum

    Youth Constitution Forum

    Four youth groups developed recommendations on civic education and participation.

    What participants proposed

    • Start civic education earlier
    • Set up school clubs
    • Hold an annual civic forum
    • Improve accessibility
    • Encourage responsible use of AI

    These are the participants’ recommendations. An annual forum was proposed; it is not a confirmed recurring event.

    {{i:youtube}}Watch the forum coveragePublished on our YouTube channel, 18 September 2026

    Looking for our 22 June podcast on the Constitution? That was a separate discussion — Democracy Beyond Elections.

  4. 22 August 2026
    Community

    Mass Sport Extravaganza, Perseverance

    A community sports day. The programme advertised football, beach handball, dominoes and volleyball.

    Registration for this event has closed.
